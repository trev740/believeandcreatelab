Privacy Policy

StreamLens Privacy Policy

StreamLens is designed to keep your camera and microphone stream local between your phone and your own computer. Advertising and purchase services operate separately from your local stream.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Overview

StreamLens lets you use your phone as a wireless camera for OBS Studio and other supported desktop workflows. The app sends video and audio from your phone to your own computer over your local Wi-Fi network.

Believe N Create Lab operates StreamLens and is responsible for its privacy practices. This policy explains how the app handles local streaming data, advertising, purchases and other information, including your privacy choices.

Your camera and microphone stream is not uploaded to our servers or shared with our advertising partners. Versions of StreamLens that support advertising use Google AdMob to help fund the Free plan.

Data Collection

StreamLens does not require a user account. Camera and microphone content is handled locally for streaming. Third-party services may process personal information for advertising and purchases as described below.

  • Camera settings, connection preferences and plan-related information may be stored on your device.
  • Advertising services may collect device, network, usage and diagnostic information.
  • Apple processes purchases and provides transaction and entitlement information so the app can unlock purchased features.
  • If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide.
  • We do not upload or store your camera or microphone stream on our servers.

Camera and Microphone

StreamLens asks for access to your camera and microphone so it can stream video and audio from your phone to your own computer.

Your camera and microphone stream is transmitted locally to your computer on the same Wi-Fi network. StreamLens does not provide your video or audio content to Google AdMob or use it to personalise advertisements.

You can change camera and microphone permissions in your device settings. Disabling these permissions may prevent the corresponding streaming features from working. Any recording or onward broadcast you configure in OBS Studio or another desktop application is governed by that application and your own settings.

Local Network

StreamLens uses your local network to discover and connect to the StreamLens desktop plugin running on your computer. Your phone and computer must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network for local streaming.

Advertising and Apple purchase services may require an internet connection. These services are separate from the local video and audio transmission.

Subscriptions and Purchases

StreamLens may offer optional subscriptions, free trials and lifetime purchases through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes payments, and StreamLens uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to determine access to paid features and restore eligible purchases.

We do not receive your payment card details. For information about Apple's handling of your personal information, please see the Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Advertising-enabled versions of StreamLens may display advertisements to users on the Free plan. The Pro plan, including eligible active Pro trials and lifetime access, is intended to provide an ad-free experience.

Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, may collect or share information such as:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location.
  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier where permitted, and other app-related identifiers.
  • Advertisement views, clicks and other advertising interactions.
  • Information about interactions with the app.
  • Crash information and performance diagnostics.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, understand usage, improve service reliability, detect invalid advertising activity and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices, device permissions and applicable law, advertisements may be personalised, non-personalised or limited, or may not be served at all.

Non-personalised advertising does not necessarily mean that no personal information is processed. Google and participating advertising providers may receive advertising-related information, subject to the applicable consent settings and legal requirements.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

StreamLens uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) in advertising-enabled versions to manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be shown a consent message before advertisements are requested. Other regional privacy messages may be available where configured and required.

Where the consent message applies, you can accept, decline or manage your choices, including permitted advertising purposes and participating providers. Where required, you can revisit your choices through the privacy options in StreamLens Settings. You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Declining advertising consent does not disable the core local streaming functionality. Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, some non-personalised or limited advertising may remain available.

Consent services may store and read privacy preferences on your device so your choices can be communicated and respected. More information is available in Google's European regulations message guidance.

Apple App Tracking Transparency

On Apple devices, tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) rules requires your permission. If a version of StreamLens requests tracking permission, you can choose whether to allow it.

You can review or change tracking permissions in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline tracking, the app must not track you across other companies' apps and websites as defined by Apple's rules. Advertising may still be available without tracking, subject to your privacy choices and applicable law.

Apple's tracking permission and advertising consent are separate choices. Neither replaces the other when both are required.

How and Why Information Is Processed

Where UK or European data protection law applies, the relevant legal basis depends on the purpose of processing:

  • Providing the service: information necessary to deliver requested app features and administer purchases may be processed to perform our contract with you.
  • Consent: we rely on your consent where required for advertising personalisation, tracking, or the storage of or access to information on your device.
  • Legitimate interests: where permitted and subject to your rights, information necessary for security, fraud prevention and support may be processed to operate and protect the service.
  • Legal obligations: information may be processed where necessary to comply with applicable law.

Google and other advertising providers explain their own processing purposes and applicable legal bases through their privacy notices and the consent interface. Providing consent for optional advertising purposes is not a condition of using the core local streaming service.

Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

StreamLens uses Apple services for in-app purchases and Google services for advertising and consent management in supported versions. Advertising providers identified through the consent interface may also process advertising-related information. These providers operate under their own applicable privacy terms and legal obligations.

Advertising-related disclosures or exchanges may qualify as a sale, sharing or targeted advertising under certain regional privacy laws, even where no payment is made for the information itself. Applicable opt-out and privacy rights are described below. Camera and microphone stream content is not shared with advertising providers.

Data Retention

StreamLens does not retain your camera or microphone stream on our servers. Locally stored preferences are retained on your device as needed to operate the app and may be removed by deleting the app or using any applicable reset options.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. Apple, Google and other service providers retain information according to their own purposes, policies and legal requirements. For details of Google's retention practices, see How Google retains data it collects.

International Data Transfers

Your local camera and microphone transmission stays on your local network. Information processed by Apple, Google and other advertising providers may be processed in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the UK or EEA.

Where applicable, international transfers must comply with relevant data protection requirements, which may include adequacy decisions or appropriate contractual safeguards. For information about Google's arrangements, see its data transfer frameworks. You may contact us for further information about applicable safeguards.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal information about you and request a copy.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information.
  • Request restriction of processing or object to certain uses.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent or opt out of applicable targeted advertising, sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Complain to a relevant data protection authority.

You can use applicable in-app privacy options or email us at believencreatelab@gmail.com to exercise your rights. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. You can also contact Apple or Google directly regarding information they control.

In the UK, you may contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In the EEA or elsewhere, you may contact your applicable local supervisory authority.

Children’s Privacy

StreamLens is a camera and streaming utility and is not specifically directed at children. The app does not include social features, messaging, public profiles or user-generated content feeds.

Advertising and consent settings must comply with applicable age-related privacy requirements. If you believe personal information about a child has been processed inappropriately through StreamLens, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
