Pocket Reactor Privacy Policy

Pocket Reactor is designed to generate and manage AI images privately on your device.

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Overview

Pocket Reactor lets you create AI-generated images from text prompts and optional source images. Image generation is performed locally on your device using AI model files downloaded by the app.

We do not upload your prompts, selected source images, generated images, or Library passcode to our servers.

Information Processed on Your Device

Pocket Reactor may process and store the following information locally on your device:

  • Text prompts and generation settings
  • Images you choose to use as input
  • Images generated by the app
  • Local Library information
  • Your Library passcode setting
  • Downloaded AI model files and temporary cache files
  • Your Pocket Reactor Pro entitlement status

This information remains on your device unless you choose to export, share, or save an image using iOS features.

AI Image Generation

AI image generation takes place locally on your device.

Your text prompts, selected images, and generated images are not sent to Believe N Create Lab or uploaded to our servers for image generation.

Model Downloads

Pocket Reactor requires an internet connection to download optional AI model files and model catalogue information. These files are downloaded from FileCloud-hosted storage and saved within the app’s local storage.

When your device connects to the model-download service, the hosting provider may process standard technical connection information, such as your IP address, request time, device or network information, and download status. This information may appear in ordinary service or security logs.

We do not use model-download requests to create advertising profiles, track you across other apps or websites, or analyse the content of your prompts and images.

Photos and Image Access

Pocket Reactor may ask for access to photos when you choose an image to use as input or save a generated image to your photo library.

The app only uses this access to perform the action you request. Selected and generated images are not uploaded to our servers.

Library Passcode

The optional Library passcode and its enabled status are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.

Believe N Create Lab cannot view, recover, or reset your Library passcode remotely.

Data Collection

Pocket Reactor does not create user accounts and does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

We do not:

  • Collect or upload your prompts or generated images
  • Sell your personal information
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Track you across apps or websites
  • Create public profiles
  • Provide social feeds or messaging features

Limited technical information may be processed by Apple when you use the App Store or make a purchase, and by the model-hosting provider when your device downloads model files.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Pocket Reactor may offer optional monthly and yearly subscriptions through Apple’s in-app purchase system.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Pocket Reactor receives the subscription product and entitlement status needed to unlock Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, and refund handling are managed through your Apple Account and the App Store.

Data Retention and Deletion

Prompts, generated images, selected images, downloaded models, passcode settings, and app preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear available cache data, or remove the app.

Deleting Pocket Reactor removes locally stored app data in accordance with iOS behaviour. Images you previously saved to your Photos library must be deleted separately from the Photos app.

Because Pocket Reactor does not create an account or upload your prompts and generated images to our servers, we generally do not hold a server copy of this content.

Technical logs processed by Apple or the model-hosting provider may be retained according to their own operational, security, and legal requirements.

Third-Party Services

Pocket Reactor uses the following external services only where required for app functionality:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit — subscriptions, purchases, restoration, and entitlement verification
  • FileCloud-hosted storage — delivery of AI model files and model catalogue information

These services may process limited technical information required to provide their services. Pocket Reactor does not use third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

Pocket Reactor is not designed to collect personal information from children.

The app does not include user accounts, public profiles, social feeds, public image sharing, or private messaging between users.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Decline photo access and continue using text-only image generation
  • Remove generated images from the Pocket Reactor Library
  • Delete images saved to the Photos app
  • Clear available temporary cache data from Settings
  • Disable the Library passcode after confirming the current passcode
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored app data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pocket Reactor’s features, services, or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
